Inside Mnemosyne

Mnemosyne is not a standard vector database. It is a biologically-inspired associative memory layer designed to give LLMs a persistent sense of history and context.

Hebbian Dynamics

Memories that are frequently retrieved together grow stronger. Unused trivia naturally decays over time, mimicking the biological process of forgetting.

Spreading Activation

Mnemosyne traverses a localized SQLite graph. A query for one topic "lights up" related nodes, surfacing associative echoes that pure text search misses.

100% Local

Your history, associations, and self-model never leave your machine. No cloud APIs, no data leaks, no subscriptions.

How It Works

LTP & LTD

Long-Term Potentiation (LTP) strengthens memory weights when a tool like memory_cite is called. Long-Term Depression (LTD) occurs during the "sleep" consolidation pass, where weak edges are pruned to maintain efficiency.

Episode Extraction

Mnemosyne doesn't just store raw text. It identifies "episodes"—significant moments like decisions, first-time meetings, or project milestones—and assigns them salience scores to ensure the most important context stays at the surface.

Synaptic Scaling

To prevent weights from bloating and overwhelming the system, Mnemosyne performs synaptic scaling, normalizing the total weight across the graph so retrieval remains balanced and fast.
